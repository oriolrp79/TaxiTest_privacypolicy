# Privacy Policy for TaxiTestBCN

**Last updated:** October 19, 2026

TaxiTestBCN ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and information sources when you use our mobile application.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
TaxiTestBCN is designed as an educational tool to help candidates prepare for the metropolitan taxi credential exam in Barcelona.

- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, physical location, or device identifiers).
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to use the application.
- **No Tracking or Analytics:** The app does not include third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs, or user profiling tools.

### 2. Government Information and Non-Affiliation Disclaimer
- **Non-Affiliation:** TaxiTestBCN is an independent educational study application. It is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, authorized by, or in any way officially connected to the Àrea Metropolitana de Barcelona (AMB), the Institut Metropolità del Taxi (IMET), or any public or governmental entity.
- **Official Information Sources:** The contents, street lists, and points of interest referenced in this app are based on publicly available documentation provided by the official taxi regulatory body (AMB / IMET). You can verify official exam calls, regulations, and source materials directly at:  
https://taxi.amb.cat/ca/professionals-del-sector/treballar-al-taxi/acces-a-la-professio

### 3. Local Data and Network Connectivity
- **Bundled Study Content:** The street database, points of interest, and vector map graphics are bundled locally within the app installation files. Once launched, study sessions and map exploration do not consume mobile data.
- **Internet Connectivity Requirement:** An active internet connection is required upon launching the application solely to verify system security, validate service integrity, and manage the trial period mechanism.
- **Local Settings:** Any test progress, calibration settings, or layer toggles are stored strictly locally on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.

### 4. Google Play Services and In-App Purchases
The application integrates native Google Play services to ensure secure distribution and maintenance:
- **In-App Billing (Google Play Billing API):** Financial transactions for the full-access purchase are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not process, receive, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking details.
- **In-App Updates:** The app utilizes Google Play's native In-App Update API to check for critical updates. 

Both services are operated directly by Google LLC in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### 5. Third-Party Services and Advertising
The app does not display third-party advertisements and does not share data with external marketing or analytic platforms.

### 6. Age Restrictions and Children's Privacy
The application is intended for adult candidates and users aged 16 and older. We do not direct our services to children under the age of 16, nor do we knowingly collect any data from minors.

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be reflected directly on this page with an updated revision date.
