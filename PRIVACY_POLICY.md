# Privacy Policy for TaxiTestBCN

**Last updated:** September 28, 2026

TaxiTestBCN ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and information sources when you use our mobile application.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
TaxiTestBCN is designed as an educational tool to help candidates prepare for the metropolitan taxi credential exam in Barcelona.

* **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers).
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to use the application.
* **No Tracking or Analytics:** The app does not include third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs, or user profiling tools.

### 2. Government Information and Non-Affiliation Disclaimer
* **Non-Affiliation:** TaxiTestBCN is an independent educational study application. It is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, authorized by, or in any way officially connected to the Àrea Metropolitana de Barcelona (AMB), the Institut Metropolità del Taxi (IMET), or any public or governmental entity.
* **Official Information Sources:** The contents, street lists, and points of interest referenced in this app are based on publicly available documentation provided by the official taxi regulatory body (AMB / IMET). You can verify official exam calls, regulations, and source materials directly at:  
https://taxi.amb.cat/ca/professionals-del-sector/treballar-al-taxi/acces-a-la-professio

### 3. Permissions and Local Data
* **Offline Operation:** The street database, points of interest, and map graphics are bundled locally within the app installation files. The core features of the app function completely offline without requiring access to your contacts, camera, storage files, or precise GPS location.
* **Local Settings:** Any test progress, calibration settings, or layer toggles are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.

### 4. Google Play In-App Updates
The application utilizes Google Play's native In-App Update API (`in_app_update`) to notify you when a new version of the app is available. This service is managed directly by Google Play Services according to Google's standard privacy policies. We do not receive or store any personal information through this update mechanism.

### 5. Third-Party Services
The app does not display third-party advertisements and does not share data with third-party analytical platforms.

### 6. Age Restrictions and Children's Privacy
The application is intended for users aged 16 and older. We do not direct our services to individuals under the age of 16, nor do we knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under 16. If you are under 16 years of age, please do not use this application.

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
